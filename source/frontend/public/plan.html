How are times and distances calculated?

Stops start from 08:00, 12:00 or 18:00 based on your chosen part of day. We add an estimated visit duration and travel buffer between stops. Edit any stop to set your own time.

Distances are straight-line estimates. Google Maps provides the actual route. Opening hours are reference information, not live availability. Entry fees exclude food and transport.
